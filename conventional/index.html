Know your options

How this
program works.

A flexible starting point

Lone Star Financing offers fixed-rate and adjustable-rate conventional options. The right structure depends on your payment goals and how long you expect to keep the loan.

Low down payment options

Some qualified purchase borrowers may be eligible for 3%–5% down. Occupancy, loan type, credit, and underwriting determine what is available.

Understand mortgage insurance

A 20% down payment generally avoids private mortgage insurance. With less down, compare the cost of PMI and the requirements for eventual cancellation.

Make an informed choice

Common questions

A clearer
starting point.

Is a conventional loan only for first-time buyers?

No. Conventional financing can serve first-time and repeat buyers, subject to the property and loan requirements.

Can I buy with less than 20% down?

Some eligible buyers can. We will compare the down payment, PMI, payment, and overall cost rather than looking at the down payment alone.

How do I know whether I need a jumbo loan?

We will compare your loan amount with the applicable conforming limit and review jumbo options if needed.
