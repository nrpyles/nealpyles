Know your options

How this
program works.

Match financing to the property

Lone Star Financing lists multifamily, retail, office, industrial, and hospitality financing. The asset type and business plan shape underwriting.

Plan for the transaction

Published options include acquisition, refinance, bridge, construction, and eligible SBA-backed structures. Different paths address different timing and repayment needs.

Review the deal economics

Property income, expenses, occupancy, leases, ownership, borrower experience, and the exit plan can all influence the available terms.

Make an informed choice

What to
consider.

  • Commercial loan structures differ from a standard home mortgage.
  • Loan amount, leverage, amortization, term, recourse, and prepayment terms vary by property and lender.
  • Construction and bridge financing require a clear project or exit plan.

Common questions

A clearer
starting point.

Is a commercial loan the same as a DSCR rental loan?

No. A residential DSCR loan and a commercial real estate loan may evaluate different property types and structures.

Can I discuss a purchase or a refinance?

Both are listed in Lone Star Financing’s commercial offering. We will review the property and transaction.

What should I bring to a first discussion?

Start with the property, requested amount, current income and expenses, ownership, and the project timeline.
