Common questions

A clearer
starting point.

Can I finance more than four units?

Crestmark advertises selected programs for 5–8 unit buildings as well as 2–4 unit properties. Availability depends on the building and underwriting.

Is a personal guarantee still possible?

Yes. Property-income qualification and entity ownership do not automatically remove personal guarantee requirements.

Can I use the program for a refinance?

Crestmark lists purchase and refinance uses. The building’s income, current debt, and proposed structure need review.
