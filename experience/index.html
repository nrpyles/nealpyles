A little about me

Home is personal.
So is the advice.

North Texas is home for me. Growing up here and attending UNT are part of my story, and Frisco is where I’m based today. My local roots are a starting point for helping people buy, refinance, and invest throughout Texas.

When we talk, I want to understand what you’re trying to do: your timeline, the payment you’re comfortable with, and the questions keeping you from taking the next step. Those details matter as much as the loan program.

I work with Lone Star Financing and 25+ lending partners to explore options that fit your qualifications. My role is to help you understand the numbers, the tradeoffs, and what happens next—so you can make an informed decision.
