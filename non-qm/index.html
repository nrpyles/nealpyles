Common questions

A clearer
starting point.

Are bank statement loans for self-employed borrowers?

They can be an option for eligible self-employed borrowers whose tax returns do not fully reflect the income used for qualification.

Is non-QM the same as a DSCR loan?

DSCR is one investor-focused approach within the broader non-QM market. Owner-occupied bank statement financing uses a different review.

Do I still need credit and assets reviewed?

Yes. The lender reviews credit, available funds, property, and the applicable documentation requirements.
