Common questions

A clearer
starting point.

Can I take cash out of an investment property?

Crestmark lists cash-out DSCR refinance options. Available proceeds depend on the selected program, property value, debt, and underwriting.

Do I need traditional income qualification?

Crestmark describes a rental-income-based review instead of traditional personal-income DTI qualification. Property, credit, assets, and other documents still matter.

Should I refinance if the rate is lower?

Review costs, any payoff penalty, cash flow, new term, and your investment plan. A rate reduction alone does not establish a benefit.
