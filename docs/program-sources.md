# Program content research

Reviewed October 4, 2026 (America/Chicago).

Program data and exact public source URLs are in `content/loan-programs.json`.
Lone Star Financing's home page, loan-type directory, and 15 program/source pages were read directly. The new pages paraphrase program explanations rather than reproduce provider marketing copy. Each program page links to the relevant provider source.

Crestmark Lending became reachable during this task. Its DSCR purchase, requirements, guidelines, refinance, multifamily, renovation/rehab, jumbo, Texas, LLC, and FAQ pages were read directly. The DSCR landing page now uses those verified sources, and five dedicated pages cover refinance, multifamily, renovation, jumbo, and LLC financing. Each links to its specific source. Published ranges are described as program-specific and conditional, rather than approvals or guaranteed pricing.

Crestmark's pages show inconsistent general loan caps ($2M, $3M, and $5M+), geographic coverage statements, and refinance leverage. No blanket DSCR maximum, nationwide lending claim, or universal refinance LTV is published in the new pages. Property unit eligibility is limited to the stated program context. LLC title does not imply removal of personal guarantees.
## Content decisions

- Conventional: fixed/adjustable options, eligible low down payments, and PMI tradeoffs.
- FHA: eligible 3.5% down, primary occupancy, insurance, gifts, and assistance.
- VA: eligible zero-down financing, entitlement, funding fee, and no monthly PMI.
- Jumbo: provider-advertised options up to $3 million, with underwriting qualification.
- USDA: household-income and address eligibility; eligible zero-down financing.
- Non-QM: bank statement, 1099, asset-based, and specialty documentation routes.
- Refinance, cash-out, HELOC, and home equity: costs, payment/term changes, equity structure, and Texas rules.
- VA IRRRL and FHA Streamline: existing-loan eligibility, benefit, documentation, and costs.
- Assistance: grants versus second liens, repayment/forgiveness conditions, and program eligibility.
- Commercial: published property categories and transaction structures, without copying deal-specific leverage/approval claims.

The provider site contains conflicting cash-out waiting-period language and aggressive non-QM credit-event statements. These were not copied. Numeric conforming/county limits, rates, closing-time promises, and approval guarantees are omitted to avoid stale or contradictory claims. Eligibility is always subject to the actual underwriting rules.

Production deployment, FormSubmit recipient activation, and live mortgage-rate delivery have not been verified by this content change.
