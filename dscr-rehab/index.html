Make an informed choice

What to
consider.

  • Projected rents or after-improvement value are not automatically accepted.
  • Draw schedules, reserves, timelines, and permitted work need to be confirmed.
  • A planned refinance is not guaranteed; model costs and alternatives if the exit changes.
