Know your options

How this
program works.

Entity-compatible financing

Crestmark lists loans that can close in an eligible LLC or business entity for qualifying business-purpose rental properties.

Rental-income-based review

The property’s qualifying income is central to the review. Credit, liquidity, ownership, and the lender’s entity rules still matter.

Purchase, refinance, or scale

Published options include eligible acquisitions and refinancing for entity-held rentals. Portfolio or multi-property scenarios require additional review.

Make an informed choice

What to
consider.

  • Entity ownership does not automatically eliminate personal guarantees.
  • The lender may require organizational documents, member information, and ownership verification.
  • An LLC’s legal or tax effects are separate from the mortgage program; discuss the structure with your advisors.

Common questions

A clearer
starting point.

Can a loan close directly in an LLC?

Crestmark lists entity-compatible DSCR options. The specific entity, property, and lender rules must be reviewed.

Will I have to provide a personal guarantee?

Crestmark notes that a personal guarantee is typically required. The exact requirements depend on the program and structure.

Is this for a primary residence?

The published LLC DSCR offering is business-purpose financing for investment properties, rather than an owner-occupied primary home.
