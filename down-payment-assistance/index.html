How this

Several forms of assistance

Lone Star Financing describes grants, forgivable loans, and deferred-payment second liens. Each form has different conditions and obligations.

Eligibility comes first

Assistance may depend on income, property location, occupancy, purchase price, and sometimes first-time buyer status. Available funding can change.

Common questions

A clearer
starting point.

Is every assistance program a grant?

No. Programs can include grants, deferred loans, forgivable loans, or other second-lien structures.

Do I have to be a first-time buyer?

Some programs require it and others may serve repeat buyers. We will check the actual program rules.

Can assistance help with closing costs?

Some programs permit that, subject to eligible expenses, funding availability, and other requirements.
