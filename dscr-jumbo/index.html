Common questions

A clearer
starting point.

How much can I borrow?

Crestmark lists several high-balance ranges across its programs. We will confirm the applicable maximum and available amount for your specific property rather than use a blanket cap.

Is jumbo DSCR the same as a homebuyer jumbo loan?

No. Jumbo DSCR is investor-oriented property-income financing. An owner-occupied jumbo loan uses a different qualification path.

Are interest-only options available?

Crestmark lists eligible interest-only options. The term, later payment changes, and qualification requirements need to be reviewed.
