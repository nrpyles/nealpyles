Know your options

How this
program works.

An FHA-to-FHA option

This program is for eligible borrowers with an existing FHA-insured mortgage. It is not the route for converting a conventional loan to FHA.

Potentially less paperwork

Lone Star Financing describes reduced income documentation and no new appraisal in many cases. The transaction still has eligibility requirements and costs.

Review insurance and fees

FHA mortgage insurance, closing costs, and the new payment should be compared together when evaluating the benefit.

Make an informed choice

What to
consider.

  • Existing FHA loan, seasoning, payment history, and benefit requirements apply.
  • A streamlined review is not an automatic approval or a no-cost refinance.

Common questions

A clearer
starting point.

Can any homeowner use FHA Streamline?

No. It is for an eligible existing FHA-insured mortgage.

Is a benefit required?

Yes. The refinance must satisfy the applicable net tangible benefit requirements.

Does streamlined mean no closing costs?

No. We will review actual costs, any lender credits, and the proposed new terms.
